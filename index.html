Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding admission criteria, scholarships, and campus facilities.

AET is the official university scholarship entrance examination. Students appearing for AET can secure up to a 100% tuition waiver across all faculties. You can register directly at aet-home.vercel.app.

Applicants must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with an aggregate score of at least 60% from a recognized board, along with a valid score in ALSA Entrance Test (AET) or JEE Main.

Merit scholarships are awarded automatically during admission based on 12th board marks, entrance examination ranks, or sports achievements. Waivers range from 15% up to 100% of tuition fees.

Hostel accommodation is optional but highly recommended for first-year outstation students to foster campus integration and easy access to late-night library and lab facilities.

Our dedicated Center for Corporate Relations provides soft-skills training, mock interviews, and internships. Over 250+ top global recruiters participate in our placement drive annually.
